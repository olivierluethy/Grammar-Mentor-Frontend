Our Collaboration: AI Grammar Mentor × DataCloakAI

We proudly partner with DataCloakAI — a Swiss startup specialized in privacy-first AI solutions — on the project “AI Grammar Mentor X DataCloakAI”.

This collaboration focuses on building a secure, privacy-preserving infrastructure from the ground up. DataCloakAI's expertise in prompt anonymization ensures that your writing remains protected at every step.

How Your Data Is Protected

  • Full anonymization on the backend
    All prompts and text you submit are automatically anonymized before being processed by any AI model. No personally identifiable information leaves our secure Swiss-based environment.
  • No sensitive data sent
    Sensitive content is detected and masked or removed automatically — protecting your privacy by design.
  • Your responsibility
    While we apply strong anonymization, we recommend that you never paste highly sensitive, confidential, or personal information you would not want processed — even anonymized — through any AI service.
  • 100% Swiss-based
    Both AI Grammar Mentor and DataCloakAI are proud Swiss startups. All infrastructure, data processing, and operations remain fully within Switzerland — subject to strict Swiss data protection laws (including GDPR equivalence via the Swiss–EU adequacy decision).
