Privacy Policy

Last updated: 01.01.2025

At Grammar Mentor, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our website and AI-powered grammar checking & writing assistance service (“Service”).

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Text you submit – the content you paste or write into the editor for grammar checking
  • Technical data – IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, pages visited, time spent
  • Usage data – which features you use, correction mode/language selected, number of checks performed
  • Contact information – email address only if you voluntarily provide it (e.g. newsletter signup or support request)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service (including AI grammar analysis)
  • Process and respond to your requests or support inquiries
  • Analyze usage trends to enhance user experience
  • Communicate important updates, security notices, or service changes (rare)
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your submitted texts to train public AI models or sell/share them with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your submitted text is processed in real time and is not stored permanently on our servers after the request is completed, except for temporary caching required for service delivery.

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use essential cookies for site functionality (e.g., remembering UI preferences) and analytics cookies to understand usage patterns. You can manage your cookie preferences via your browser settings.

For more details, please see our Cookie Policy (if you create one).

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only:

  • With service providers (e.g. cloud hosting, analytics) under strict confidentiality
  • When required by law, court order, or governmental request
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Grammar Mentor, users, or the public

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@grammar-mentor.com.

We will respond to verifiable requests within the timeframes required by applicable law (e.g., 30 days under GDPR / nDSG).

7. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed in countries outside Switzerland or the EU (e.g. by cloud providers). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses) when required.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version here and update the “Last updated” date. Significant changes will be communicated via a notice on the website.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

hello@grammar-mentor.com
