Common Questions Before You Write

Do I need to be a user to email you?

No. Whether you're just curious, thinking about trying it, or have never heard of us before — feel free to write.

What if it's not about the product?

Still write! We're researchers at heart — happy to talk linguistics, language learning, AI ethics, Swiss German quirks, or whatever is on your mind.

Is there a faster way than email?

Right now, email is the most direct and personal way to reach us. We keep it simple on purpose — so nothing gets lost in tickets or chatbots.
