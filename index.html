The Real Problem With Grammar Checkers

It's not that they don't work. It's that they work too well in all the wrong ways.

😤

They interrupt your flow

You're deep in thought, crafting a sentence, and suddenly a suggestion pops up demanding your attention. Breaking concentration over and over just to dismiss things you already know are fine.

🤖

They flatten your voice

AI rewrites turn your conversational blog post into a corporate press release. Your novel's dialogue sounds like a business memo. Romance becomes technical documentation. Your personality gets erased.

🤷

They never explain anything

Red underline. "Fix this." But why? What rule did you break? How do you avoid it next time? You're left clicking "accept" on corrections you don't understand, learning nothing.

💸

They trap you with unclear pricing

"Free trial" that auto-renews. Hidden limits. Aggressive upsells blocking features you need. You just want to check your writing, not decode a billing scheme designed to maximize confusion.

What You Actually Need

A writing improvement tool that respects your intelligence and helps you learn.

✋

The freedom to ignore suggestions without friction

Sometimes you break the rules on purpose. Your dialogue is supposed to be informal. That sentence fragment is stylistic. You need a grammar checker that highlights issues but lets you dismiss them with one click and move on. No arguing. No re-suggesting the same thing. Just quiet, respectful acknowledgment that you're the writer here.

For freelance writers, novelists, and anyone who knows their own voice.

📚

Real explanations that teach you grammar rules

You're not looking for a magic button that fixes everything. You want to understand why the comma goes there, what passive voice actually means, and how to spot these issues yourself next time. An English grammar checker should be a mentor, not a servant. It should explain the rule, show examples, and help you internalize it.

For students, non-native speakers, and lifelong learners.

🎨

Your writing style, untouched

Professional editors don't rewrite your entire manuscript. They suggest fixes for actual errors and let you make the call. This AI writing assistant works the same way: it highlights grammar mistakes, offers alternatives, but never forces a rewrite. Your romance novel still sounds romantic. Your blog still sounds like you. Your dialogue still sounds human.

For creative writers, bloggers, and content creators.

Support for how you actually write

Maybe you're writing in your second language and need more context. Maybe you're switching between British and American English. Maybe your document is huge and other tools crash or refuse to load it. A multilingual grammar checker should handle real-world writing scenarios without making assumptions about how or why you're writing.

For multilingual users, students, and anyone working with large documents.

Honest pricing with no trap doors

You should know exactly what you're paying for, what's free, and what's premium. No surprise charges. No aggressive upsells blocking basic features. No auto-renewals you didn't clearly agree to. Just clear pricing from a company that values trust more than growth hacks.

For budget-conscious writers, students, and anyone tired of subscription traps.

AI when you want it, classic grammar when you don't

Why We Built This

We're a small Swiss startup that got tired of watching writing tools treat users like they don't matter. Every major grammar checker seemed designed to either take over your writing completely or nickel-and-dime you with confusing pricing.

We talked to writers. Lots of them. Freelancers who kept dismissing the same incorrect suggestions. Students who wanted to learn grammar, not just pass it off to AI. Novelists whose tools crashed on large manuscripts. Non-native speakers who needed cultural context, not just corrections. People who felt trapped by subscriptions they didn't fully understand.

The pattern was clear: people don't need more automation. They need more understanding. More control. More respect.

So we built AI Grammar Mentor as a writing improvement tool that explains grammar rules, preserves your voice, and charges honestly. We're based in Switzerland because we believe in quality, transparency, and treating users with dignity. This isn't a growth-at-all-costs company. It's a tool built by people who care about writing.
