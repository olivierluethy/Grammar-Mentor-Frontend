About AI Grammar Mentor

A Swiss research initiative redefining how people master languages through understanding, not automation.

AI Grammar Mentor is developed by a Zurich-based Swiss research group focused on optimizing real-life language acquisition and progression.

We believe that true mastery of a language — whether English, German, French or any other — comes from understanding why something is correct or incorrect, not from blindly accepting auto-corrections. Our mission is to create tools that support meaningful, long-term progress instead of offering quick fixes that leave users dependent.

Traditional grammar tools often hide the learning process behind black-box suggestions or rewrite entire sentences in a generic style. We take the opposite approach: we explain grammar rules clearly, preserve your personal voice, and help you internalize knowledge so you make fewer mistakes naturally over time.

Research-Driven from Zurich, Switzerland

Our group is based in Zurich, one of Europe’s leading hubs for linguistics, cognitive science and educational technology research. We draw inspiration from interdisciplinary work in second language acquisition, writing pedagogy, human-AI collaboration and applied linguistics.

Switzerland’s multilingual environment — with four national languages and a high proportion of international residents — gives us a unique perspective on real-world language learning challenges. We design with non-native speakers, academic writers, creative authors and professionals in mind.
